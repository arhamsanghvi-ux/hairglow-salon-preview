About

A Little Time for Yourself.
A Look That Feels Like You.

Some places give you a haircut. Others make you glad you walked in.

At Hair and Glow Salon, we believe the difference is in the details: the comfort of feeling welcome, the ease of being understood, and the satisfaction of walking out with exactly the look you had in mind. No unnecessary fuss, no feeling like just another customer. Just good people, thoughtful service, and a team that genuinely cares about getting things right.

Whether you're stopping by for your regular trim, changing up your style, or simply taking a moment for yourself, we want every visit to feel easy, personal, and worth coming back for. In a space that is clean, comfortable, and cared for, you can sit back, speak your mind, and leave the little details to us.

Because great grooming isn't just about what you see in the mirror. It's about how you feel when you look into it.

Hair and Glow Salon, Ghatkopar East. Walk in for the look. Come back for the experience.

Run by Santosh Tavare at Neelkanth Regalia, Station Road. Open seven days a week.
